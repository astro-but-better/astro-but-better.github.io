Astro-TURF is a lightweight but powerful approach to building **swappable, theme-driven Astro sites**.
It stands for:

<div class="turf-list">
  <ul>
    <li>Themeable</li>
    <li>Unified</li>
    <li>Robust</li>
    <li>Federated</li>
  </ul>
</div>

The idea is simple:
**separate the content, the engine, and the theme**—and allow each to evolve independently.

With Astro-TURF, you can:

- build multiple sites that share the same theming engine,
- swap themes without rewriting your content,
- publish federated content from multiple repositories,
- and keep everything clean, predictable, and maintainable.

This project began as an experiment and quickly revealed a gap in the Astro ecosystem:
there are many templates, but almost no **stable, long-term theming architecture**.
Astro-TURF aims to fill that missing piece.

Whether you're creating a personal site, a documentation hub, or a federated content network,
Astro-TURF gives you a structure that grows with you—not against you.
